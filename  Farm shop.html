Welcome to GreenFields Integrated Farm, located in Abuja (FCT). We practice modern Nigerian agriculture, combining traditional farming wisdom with innovative techniques. Our animals are raised healthy and naturally, while our crops are grown using organic methods. We deliver fresh throughout Abuja.

Our Livestock

Cattle (Beef & Dairy)

We raise White Fulani, Red Bororo, and improved breeds for beef production. Our cattle graze on quality pasture with supplementary feeding for tender, quality beef.

Goat (Chevon)

West African Dwarf and Red Sokoto goats raised naturally. Popular for pepper soup, asun, nkwobi, and special occasions. Fresh or live delivery available.

Broilers & Layers

Healthy broiler chickens and point-of-lay pullets. Our chickens are raised with quality feed, producing tender meat and fresh eggs daily.

Sheep (Ram & Ewe)

Yankasa, Balami, and Uda breeds perfect for celebrations, Ileya (Eid-el-Kabir), weddings, and special events. Live or processed available.

Local & Muscovy Ducks

Hardy breeds raised around our farm ponds. Excellent for grilling and special occasions. Rich flavor and good meat-to-bone ratio.

Turkey

Premium turkeys for Christmas, New Year, and celebrations. Broad-breasted and local varieties. Pre-order for festive seasons.

Pigs

Large White and Duroc breeds raised in clean, spacious pens. Fresh pork cuts available for our diverse customer base.

Local Chickens

Native Nigerian chickens (local fowl) raised free-range. Perfect for pepper soup, traditional cooking. Tougher meat with superior flavor.

About GreenFields Farm

Our Story

Established in 2020, GreenFields Integrated Farm is a family-owned agricultural enterprise located in Abuja. We are passionate about providing Nigerians with access to fresh, healthy, and naturally-raised livestock and farm produce.

Our farm practices sustainable and organic farming methods. We believe in raising our animals naturally with organic feed, no hormones, and plenty of space to roam. Our crops are grown using organic methods, ensuring you get the freshest, most nutritious produce.

Frequently Asked Questions

How fresh is the meat?
All our meat is freshly processed on order. We don't store pre-cut meat. Live animals are delivered fresh or can be processed on request within 24 hours of order.
Do you deliver live animals?
Yes! We deliver live goats, rams, chickens, and turkeys. Perfect for celebrations, Sallah, and special occasions. 24-48 hours notice required.
Can I visit the farm?
Absolutely! Farm visits are welcome by appointment. Contact us on WhatsApp to schedule your visit and see our operations firsthand.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept bank transfer, USSD payment, and cash on delivery. For bank transfers, we provide account details at checkout. Payment confirmation via WhatsApp required.
Do you offer bulk discounts?
Yes! For orders above ₦100,000, contact us directly for special pricing. We cater to restaurants, hotels, and events.
What's your return policy?
We guarantee quality. If you're not satisfied with your order, contact us within 24 hours for resolution. We stand behind our products 100%.

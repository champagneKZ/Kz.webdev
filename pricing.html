Common Questions

How long does it take to build my website?
Landing pages take 3-5 days, business websites take 1-2 weeks, and e-commerce sites take 2-4 weeks. I'll give you an exact timeline after discussing your specific needs.
Do I need to provide content and images?
Yes, you'll need to provide your text, product info, and images. I'll guide you on exactly what I need. Need help with content? I offer content writing services for ₦20,000.
Can I manage the website myself after launch?
With e-commerce Pro and Premium packages, yes! You get an admin panel where you can add/edit products and manage orders. I'll train you on how to use it. Other packages require my help for updates (or subscribe to maintenance).
What if I need changes after launch?
All packages include post-launch support (1 week to 1 month). After that, minor tweaks are usually free. Major changes are charged based on time required, typically ₦5,000-20,000 depending on complexity.
Is hosting included?
Hosting is separate and costs ₦15,000-35,000 per year depending on your needs. I can set everything up for you or work with your existing hosting. I'll recommend the best option for your budget.
Can I upgrade my package later?
Absolutely! Start small and upgrade as your business grows. I'll credit what you've already paid toward the upgraded package.
